# SCServo

Feetech's library for their serial bus servos (the arm's ST3215, ST3215-HS
and ST3235), as Waveshare ships it with the General Driver for Robots
(`library.properties`: SCSservo 1.0.1, "FT&WS", https://github.com/waveshare/).
It is not in the PlatformIO registry, so it is kept here as sources,
unchanged. It came without a licence of its own; it is not covered by this
repository's MIT licence.
